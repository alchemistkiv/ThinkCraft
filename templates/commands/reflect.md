# reflect

Use this command after a plan, design, or decision exists and needs a harder second pass.

## What to do

- Restate what is being reviewed.
- Inspect assumptions, scoring quality, and confidence levels.
- Look for gaps, bias, premature closure, and false precision.
- Separate strong conclusions from weak ones.
- Suggest corrections or follow-up work.

## Output

- Review target
- Findings
- Weak assumptions or scorecard issues
- Corrective recommendations
- Suggested next action
